Real estate, credit, commodities, and equities are all moving on-chain. We analyze the $500 trillion opportunity and the infrastructure stack needed to make institutional-grade tokenization a reality.

## Why Now

Three macro forces have converged to make RWA tokenization viable for the first time: regulatory clarity in major jurisdictions, the maturation of on-chain compliance tooling, and a generation of institutional investors who have grown comfortable holding digital assets.

BlackRock's BUIDL fund crossed $500M in AUM within months of launch. Franklin Templeton's money market fund is live on Stellar. These aren't experiments — they're the opening moves of a multi-decade transition.

## The Infrastructure Gap

The biggest bottleneck isn't regulatory or institutional demand — it's infrastructure. Tokenization platforms need to handle:

- **Legal wrapper creation**: Mapping real-world legal structures to on-chain representations
- **Token issuance**: Creating tokens with embedded transfer restrictions and compliance rules
- **KYC/AML at the wallet level**: Ensuring only verified participants can hold and trade tokens
- **Dividend distribution**: Automated on-chain payment of yields, rents, and coupons
- **Secondary market liquidity**: Enabling price discovery and trading for traditionally illiquid assets

VaultMesh, our portfolio company, is building this full stack.

## The Market Opportunity

The total addressable market for tokenization is staggering:

- **Real estate**: $326T globally, with less than 0.01% tokenized
- **Fixed income**: $130T in bonds, with tokenized treasuries just crossing $1B
- **Private credit**: $1.5T market growing 15% annually
- **Commodities**: $120T in tradeable commodities

Even capturing 1% of these markets represents a $5T+ opportunity. The infrastructure providers who enable this transition will capture enormous value.

## Our Investment Framework

We evaluate RWA projects across four dimensions:

1. **Regulatory moat**: Does the team have genuine regulatory expertise and relationships?
2. **Institutional traction**: Are real institutions using the platform, not just crypto-native teams?
3. **Full-stack vs. modular**: Does the platform handle the complete lifecycle, or just one piece?
4. **Chain strategy**: Is the team chain-agnostic, or locked into a single ecosystem?

The winners in this space will be the ones who understand both TradFi and DeFi deeply enough to bridge the gap.
